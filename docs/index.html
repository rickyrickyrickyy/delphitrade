Quick answers

Deposits, fees, and payouts — the questions new traders ask first.

How do I add funds to my Delphi account?
Top up directly with your credit/debit card, or link your web3 wallet and deposit from practically any major chain, including Ethereum, Solana, BNB and Base.
Will I have to pay any fees?
Deposits and withdrawals are free, and markets created by Delphi are also free to trade. User-created markets may charge a small fee — you'll always see the exact cost before confirming your trade.
How fast can I get my winnings?
Claim your winnings as soon as a market is resolved. Resolution is targeted within 24 hours for all markets; disputes may take longer to resolve fairly.
