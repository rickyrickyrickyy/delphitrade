What is a prediction market?
A market where you trade on the outcome of future events. Prices reflect the crowd's probability — when the event resolves, correct shares pay out.
What is a share?
Shares represent a position on Yes or No for a given market. They can be bought, sold, or held to resolution depending on the market rules.
Can I create my own market?
User-created markets may be available depending on platform rules. Fees and parameters are shown before you confirm.
When can I claim my winnings?
After the market resolves and outcomes are finalized, you can claim according to the resolution timeline shown in the product.
Which coins can I use to trade on Delphi?
The live app supports deposits across major chains (e.g. Ethereum, Solana, BNB, Base). This demo does not connect to wallets or contracts.
How do I add funds?
Top up with card or connect a web3 wallet and bridge from supported networks — same messaging as the marketing site.
Are there fees?
Deposits and withdrawals are free on the live product; Delphi-created markets are free to trade. Always confirm fees on the trade preview screen.
